Native audio isolation

Use the native samples for the baseline check. The optional silent-loop test uses a separate Web Audio session with the native player paused.

  1. Load a source below, then press the audio player’s Play button.
  2. Lock the phone, pause from the lock screen, wait at least 10 seconds, then press Play once.
  3. Compare the HTTP sample with a saved song.

1. Native MP3 and AAC audio

Quiet 60-second tones, served as complete files with byte-range support. MP3 and AAC use different decoders from the original WAV test. Requires a server connection.

2. Your downloaded audio

Read directly from this device’s existing storage. Nothing is uploaded or modified.

3. Paused icon with a silent loop

Start the tone, lock the phone, and pause it from the lock screen. Wait ten seconds and press Play once while still locked. Repeat the pause and resume without reopening the app.

Choose a source. No audio starts automatically.
